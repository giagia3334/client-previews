What we found

All comparisons use organic views (total views minus views from ads), so boosted posts don’t skew the result.

A repost usually earns less than the original

The typical public repeat posting got -22% organic views compared with the same video’s 1st posting (13 of 49 did better). 2nd postings: -21% (n=42). 3rd and later: -50% (n=7).

…but it still adds reach

The 45 videos earned 7,267,596 organic views on their 1st postings. Their 60 repeat postings added another 2,434,475.

Compared with a typical new Reel

Against the median organic views of other Reels posted within ±30 days, 1st postings ran +16% and repeat postings ran +4%.

Identical vs. re-edited reposts

Public postings only. Identical re-uploads: -27% (n=39). Same video with a new first 1–3 seconds: -11% (n=3). Small text/trim edits: -27% (n=6).

11 repeat postings aren’t on the public profile

They don’t appear in the profile’s Reels grid; their views range from 2 to 86,231. They are likely trial Reels (shown to non-followers first) or posts removed from the grid, so they are reported separately: median -100% vs the 1st posting. Including them, all repeat postings: -32% (n=60).

How this was measured

Videos were matched by comparing actual video frames, not thumbnails or captions. Every group was then confirmed by eye. Details are at the end of the report.

Data sources and accuracy

Sources

  • Views, organic/ads split, reach and post list: Meta Business Suite › Insights › Content (read-only), snapshot taken Nov 5, 2026, 2:45 AM Utah time. These are lifetime figures as Meta reports them and will keep rising, mostly on recent posts.
  • Publish date and time and public links: Instagram’s own post data for the 267 Reels that appear on the public profile, shown in Utah time (Mountain). For the 17 Reels that aren’t on the public profile, the time comes from Business Suite.
  • Paid-only ad posts that never appeared on the profile (7) are left out.

How videos were matched

  • All 284 Reels were loaded from the actual video files. 12 frames were sampled from each (0.5 s to 40 s), and every Reel was compared with every other Reel.
  • Every group was then checked by eye with frames side by side, plus length to a tenth of a second. Captions and thumbnails were not used to decide a match.
  • Repeat postings are labelled “identical re-upload”, “new opening” (the first 1–3 seconds differ) or “small edit” (on-screen text or trim).
  • Videos that share only some footage with another (a different edit of the same patient, a common intro clip) are not counted as reposts. They are listed below for transparency.

Limits

  • Postings under 7 days old are shown but left out of the averages, because their views are still growing.
  • Averages in “What we found” are medians over public repeat postings unless stated; n = number of postings behind a figure. Small groups (n under 10) are directional only.
  • “vs 1st posting” compares lifetime totals, so older postings have had more time to collect views.
  • The 12-month window is Nov 5, 2025 – Nov 4, 2026 (Utah time). To find earlier postings, all 115 public Reels from Nov 2024 – Nov 2025 were compared with this year’s Reels the same way (12 frames each). Older Reels that were never on the public profile, and anything before Nov 2024, were not checked.
  • Instagram plays (used only in the “before the window” section) are the public counts on each post and include plays from ads.
