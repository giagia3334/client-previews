Seven steps for the end of the year

New patients fell in 2026 because Instagram patients fell, from 28 in January to 3 in September. Google held up and brought its best month when it had the most budget. We recommend tracking every caller by name, giving each campaign its own page, putting more behind Search, and using the warm audience we already have before the year ends.

The seven steps

  1. Track every caller and form by name. Standalone landing pages with their own tracking numbers, and a monthly list of names and statuses.
  2. "New Year, New Smile" for out-of-state patients. Three LUCAsmile cases with the trip covered, shown to our warm audience outside Utah.
  3. A LUCAsmile-only P-Max. $30 a day, our best videos, counting form leads only.
  4. A 30-second video across Utah. Non-skippable, ending with the consult offer and price.
  5. A year-end offer for Utah. A $79 consult instead of $199, plus a year of LUCAcare.
  6. Exact-match Search for missing services. Implants, All-on-4, emergency visits and Invisalign.
  7. Stop reposting Reels. Each repost earns fewer views than the first posting.

Scale it step by step

June is one month and the numbers are small, and Demand Gen also grew that month. We'd raise Search in steps and judge each step on verified new patient calls.

Step 7 · Instagram

Stop reposting Reels

We looked at every Reel from the last 12 months on @drjordandavis_ to see what reposting does. 35% of Reels were reposts (99 of 284), and only 13 of 49 reposts beat the original.

Views compared with the same video's first posting

Typical result, organic views only, ads excluded
1st posting100%
2nd posting79%-21%
3rd posting or later50%-50%
Repost from the year before27%-73%
What we foundWhat it means
1. Fewer views each timeMeta shows a video it has already shown less often in recommendations. The 2nd posting gets about 79% of the views, the 3rd about half.
2. Still some free reachA repost still reaches people who missed it, so it isn't wasted. It just earns less than a new video would.
3. A possible cost to the channelWe can't prove this, but if Meta reads weaker reposts as lower interest in the account, it may show new videos to fewer people too.

Our recommendation: post new videos or real re-edits in place of reposts.
