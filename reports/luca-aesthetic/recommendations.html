Step 7

Stop reposting Reels

Organic views compared with the same video's first posting. Ads excluded.

1. Fewer views each time

Meta shows a video it has already shown less often in recommendations. The 2nd posting gets about 79% of the views, the 3rd about half.

2. Still some free reach

A repost still reaches people who missed it, so it isn't wasted. It just earns less than a new video would.

3. A possible cost to the channel

We can't prove this, but if Meta reads weaker reposts as lower interest in the account, it may show new videos to fewer people too.

Our recommendation: post new videos or real re-edits in place of reposts.
